This application is still being improved

Some features may produce incomplete or inaccurate results. Review all suggestions before sending messages or publishing captions. Do not depend on this tool as your only source of guidance in serious or sensitive situations.

Message analysis limitations

Tone and intensity scores are estimates based on language patterns. They do not reliably reveal a person's true feelings, intentions, or the full context of a conversation. Review every suggested rewrite before sending it.

Caption accuracy and responsibility

Captions are based on the details you provide. The tool does not independently verify names, dates, locations, quotations, statistics, or other claims, and it does not automatically inspect photographs. Check facts and context before publishing.

What this tool cannot do

The assistant does not provide independent fact-checking, professional counselling, emergency response, or guaranteed emotional understanding. Seek appropriate human or professional help when a situation requires it.

Protect your personal information

Avoid entering passwords, PINs, financial information, or sensitive personal details. Saved message history is stored in this browser and may be accessible to someone who can use your device or browser profile.
